### 🔊 Music & Voice: Smart Auto-Ducking

Nexa intelligently manages music volume when you interact with it:

| State | Music Volume | What's Happening |
|-------|-------------|------------------|
| **Passive Listening** | 🔊 Full (100%) | Waiting for "Nexa" wake word |
| **Wake Word Detected** | 🔉 Ducked (8%) | "Nexa" heard, listening for command |
| **You're Speaking** | 🔉 Ducked (8%) | Recording your command |
| **Nexa Responding** | 🔉 Ducked (8%) | Speaking the response |
| **Thinking/Processing** | 🔊 Full (100%) | AI processing your request |
| **Response Complete** | 🔊 Full (100%) | Ready for next command |

**How It Works:**

1. **Wake Word Mode (Default)**: Music plays at full volume while Nexa passively listens for "Nexa"
2. **When You Say "Nexa"**: Music immediately ducks to 8% so Nexa can hear you clearly
3. **During Your Command**: Music stays ducked while you speak
4. **While Nexa Responds**: Music remains ducked so you can hear the response
5. **After Response**: Music returns to full volume automatically

**Tips:**
- If music seems too quiet, check that wake word mode is enabled ("Enable wake word")
- In direct mode (no wake word), music ducks whenever you speak
- Volume ducking is temporary — your music volume settings are preserved
- Works with any audio source: Spotify, YouTube, local files, etc.

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Multi-Step Commands
Chain commands together:
- "Open Chrome and maximize it"
- "Set volume to 50 and brightness to 80"
- "List my games, then launch the first one"

### Context Awareness
Use pronouns after previous commands:
- "Open Chrome" → "Maximize it" (maximizes Chrome)
- "List my games" → "Play the third one" (plays game #3)
- "Open Notepad" → "Close it" (closes Notepad)

### Natural Variations
These all work the same:
- "What time is it?" = "Tell me the time" = "Time please"
- "Open Chrome" = "Launch Chrome" = "Start Chrome"
- "Volume up" = "Increase volume" = "Turn it up"

### Wake Word Tips
Nexa recognizes many variations:
- "Nexa" / "Next" / "Lexa" / "Nexus" / "Extra" all work
- Speak clearly but naturally
- Wait for the acknowledgment beep before giving your command

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "I don't recognize that app" | Say "List installed apps" to see exact names |
| "Could not find game" | Say "List my games" first |
| "Music folder empty" | Add songs to `C:\Users\[You]\Music` |
| Nexa not responding | Say "Start listening" |
| Too many false triggers | Say "Enable wake word" |
| Wake word not detected | Try "Next" or "Lexa" instead |
| Music ducks without saying Nexa | Enable wake word mode: "Enable wake word" |
| Music too quiet during listening | Check that wake word is enabled (passive mode = full volume) |
| Music doesn't return to full volume | Say "Nexa, resume music" or wait a moment |
